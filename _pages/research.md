My work spans biomedical machine learning, biometrics, computer vision, physiological-signal processing, and reproducible research software.

## ECG Biometrics Benchmarking

**ECG-biometrics-bench** is a research framework for reproducible benchmarking of ECG-biometric systems. The work focuses on standardized experimental workflows, heterogeneous physiological-signal datasets, biometric evaluation protocols, preprocessing, model training, and systematic comparison.

The associated solo-authored paper, **“ECG-biometrics-bench: A Unified Framework for Reproducible Benchmarking of ECG Biometrics,”** is available as a 2026 arXiv preprint and is currently under review.

[View the arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01548)

## Ocular Computer Vision and Pupillometry

My current research collaboration at Vrije Universiteit Brussel involves computer vision for ocular analysis from RGB video. The work considers eye-region analysis, pupil localization and size estimation, temporal tracking, gaze-related measurements, and reliability under changes in illumination, viewing conditions, head movement, and image quality.

## Motor-Imagery EEG and Brain-Computer Interfaces

My M.Sc. research focused on classification of motor-imagery EEG signals using deep-learning and signal-processing methods. This work included physiological-signal preprocessing, feature extraction, model development, and comparative evaluation.

A related conference paper investigated transfer-learning-based motor-imagery classification using convolutional neural networks.

## Real-Time Road-Scene Computer Vision

In applied computer-vision work with the Road Traffic Injury Research Center, I worked on roadside-video pipelines for vehicle detection, temporal tracking, speed estimation, and inter-vehicle distance measurement.

The project involved data collection and annotation, preprocessing, model development, validation, robustness analysis, and operation under challenging conditions including illumination changes, rain, shadows, and partial occlusion.
